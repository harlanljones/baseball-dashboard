## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored-for-this-product**, with generic edges at the emotional boundaries. The linescore renders as a physical ballpark scoreboard (field-green plate, inset inning cells, gold current-inning tick); Barlow Condensed eyebrows with gold scoreboard ticks recur across every section; prop vocabulary is native ("leans," O/U prices, PY/P3D workload). The three-factor transparency console could not transplant to a generic SaaS dashboard. Where specificity sags: error/loading/empty states are category-generic dashed-border cards with no brand voice, the weight console reads as stock settings UI, and the slate's "Best leans" list is typographically flat beside the scoreboard language around it.

**Deterministic scan:** source CLI scan exit 0, zero findings. Runtime DOM overlay disagreed — game page flagged flat-type-hierarchy (computed sizes {12px×195, 14px×1232, 16px×295, 20px×9}) and nested-cards ×10 (independently corroborated: 12 nested card pairs on game page). False positives verified and discarded: all 7 "text-occlusion" hits were the overlay's own badges; 26 "em-dash-overuse" hits are table null-value placeholders counted as prose punctuation; props "147-char lines" could not be reproduced (widest visible leaf ≈82 chars).

## Overall Impression

Strong bones and a real identity — but the finish layer that separates MVP from product is missing exactly where decisions happen: the protagonist number (lean score) is the least legible element in day mode, evidence collapses to one line at the moment of action, the core research interaction (sorting) is mouse-only, and the session ends on dense reference tables instead of the decision. Fix the decision moments and this reads as a shipped product.

## What's Working

1. **The linescore is identity, not decoration** (Linescore.tsx): field-green container, LED-gutter separation, tabular mono, sr-only caption — brand carried by a data component.
2. **Evidence-first honesty is structural**: Model/Edge/Value bars with plain-language help, both sides of O/U prices shown, small samples left uncolored below 10 PA, BABIP deliberately ungraded — restraint as a trust device.
3. **Typography discipline for data**: `.nums` tabular figures, Plex Mono numerals, consistent eyebrow rhythm across 20+ components; every table has an sr-only caption.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P0] Color carries meaning with no redundancy — and the palette contradicts itself.**
   Stat grading is color-only (hot-red=good/cold-blue=bad) with **no legend anywhere**, while the props board grades green-best/gold-mid/clay-low, marks Over=cold-blue/Under=clay, and clay also means *error*. PRODUCT.md: direction/score/state must never rely on color alone.
   Fix: persistent micro-legend; pair cell colors with ▲/▼ glyphs; unify "goodness" to one encoding; reserve clay for errors.
   Command: `/impeccable clarify`
2. **[P0] Table sorting is mouse-only.**
   Both sort implementations attach bare `onClick` to `<th>` — no button, no keyboard path, no `aria-sort`. Sorting is *the* core interaction across Matchups/Bullpen/Sabermetrics.
   Fix: real `<button>` inside `<th>`, `aria-sort`, visible pressed state; unify the two drifted implementations.
   Command: `/impeccable harden`
3. **[P1] Gold hero numbers fail contrast in day mode.**
   Lean score `text-gold` (#d9a13b) on white/paper ≈ 2.3:1; same for CTA and slider percentages. Dark mode fine (~9:1). Pervasive `text-ink/45–60` at `text-xs` also sits below AA.
   Fix: darker gold *text* token for light surfaces (keep bright gold for ticks/badges on dark); lift dimmed-xs text to AA.
   Command: `/impeccable colorize`
4. **[P1] Evidence and tier language vanish at the decision moment.**
   PropRow renders only `evidence[0]`; PropsSidebar — holding full evidence and plain-language tiers ("Strong over / Lean over / Neutral") — is never rendered by any route (dead component).
   Fix: expandable full evidence per row, reinstate tier chips beside scores, deep-link Best Leans rows to the specific player/market.
   Command: `/impeccable clarify`
5. **[P2] Game page has no research hierarchy; type scale is flat.**
   Eight equal-weight sections in one scroll, identical eyebrow sizing; ends on roster WAR tables (peak-end fatigue); nested card-in-card chrome ×10.
   Fix: rank sections by prop relevance, demote Season series/Game log behind disclosure, restore type-scale steps, flatten nesting, end near the decision.
   Command: `/impeccable distill` + `/impeccable typeset`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power bettor):** Best Leans links land at top of /props — he re-scans dozens of rows to relocate the clicked lean (no anchors); weights reset nightly though pane width persists; PY/P3D definitions hover-only (invisible on touch); cross-team comparison relies on one banner row.

**Sam (accessibility-dependent):** cannot sort any table by keyboard — excluded from the core loop; quartile coloring has no text/icon alternative nor legend; `text-ink/45–60` @ text-xs below AA; no `prefers-reduced-motion` handling anywhere (pulsing live dot, skeletons, arrow translate all unconditional).

**Casey (mobile):** Factor bars stay fixed `grid-cols-3` inside narrow rows (<100px/bar at 360px); 6+ independently scrolling `min-w-max` table strips stack vertically on game page; vs-pitcher back-link says "← All games", losing the return path.

## Minor Observations

- Open-Meteo attributed twice on one view (footer + BallparkWeather footer)
- Linescore current inning marked by gold color alone — needs weight/glyph redundancy
- Raw `error.message` surfaced in user-facing copy on both error pages
- Two sortable-header implementations drifted visually (padding/hover tones)
- BestLeansSection hardcodes 40/35/25 instead of importing DEFAULT_WEIGHTS — drift risk
- "Games" title when browsing past dates loses friendly framing; date is secondary-styled
- Weather `deemphasize` opacity stacks onto already-low-contrast text
- `.nums` omitted in RosterStatsTable (font-mono compensates)
- Empty-state voice is fine but brandless next to the rest

## Questions to Consider

- If the lean score is the protagonist, why does it get the weakest ink on the page — and could your sharpest user say whether 72.4 is confident or noise?
- Red-means-good works if the scoreboard metaphor teaches it; nothing teaches it. Is hot/cold worth a legend, or should goodness speak in one accent everywhere?
- What would have to be the *last* thing a bettor sees for the session to end at the moment of decision rather than mid-reference-data?
