# Code of conduct

This project is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free environment for
everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting responsibility for impact.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Help keep discussions focused on improving the project.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, sustained
disruption, and personal attacks are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently block participants when behavior
is inappropriate. Enforcement decisions should prioritize safety, context, and
proportionality. Platform-level abuse can also be reported through
[GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, and other
spaces where someone is representing the project.
